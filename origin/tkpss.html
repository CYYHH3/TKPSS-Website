校友重聚日

Shoot for the moon. Even if you miss, you'll land among the stars.

Nothing is impossible to a willing heart.

If you can dream it, you can do it.

If you can imagine it, you can achieve it; if you can dream it, you can become it.

Hope sees the invisible, feels the intangible and achieves the impossible.

Your future depends on many things, but mostly on you.

To succeed, we must first believe that we can.

Speaking without thinking is shooting without aiming.

Without goals, and plans to reach them, you are like a ship that has set sail with no destination.

Knowledge is power.

Teachers open the door, but you must enter by yourself.

Doubt is the beginning, not the end. of wisdom.

Logical reasoning brings you from A to B. Imagination brings you everywhere.

The power of imagination makes us infinite.

The man who does not read has no advantage over the man who cannot read.

Politeness costs nothing and gains everything.

Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishments.

A heart filled with anger has no room for love.

Wisdom in the mind is better than money in the hand.

Good friends are like stars, you don't always see them but you know they're always there.

The good life is one inspired by love and guided by knowledge.

Love is the only force capable of transforming an enemy to a friend.

In this life we cannot always do great things. But we can do small things with great love.

The best and most beautiful things in life cannot be seen, not touched, but are felt in the heart.

Great minds discuss ideas; average minds discuss events; small minds discuss people.

Leadership is the special quality which enables people to stand up and pull the rest of us over the horizon.

Love cures people – both the ones who give it and the ones who receive it.

When one door of happiness closes, another opens; but often we look so long at the closed door that we do not see the one which has been opened for us.

Storms make trees take deeper roots.
